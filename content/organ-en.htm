We manufacture complete digital organs as well as individual components for building MIDI consoles (tables, pedalboards, and manual assemblies). The construction is modular, allowing the entire console to be easily disassembled and moved without any problems. At the same time, the equipment of each instrument can be customized according to individual requirements. We produce instruments on a custom basis and there is no problem accommodating special requirements for artistic, material, and technical solutions.

For sound generation, we exclusively use the so-called sampling technology. In practice, this works by recording sound samples (samples several seconds long) of all pipes of certain real pipe organs. These samples are then played back in real time using specialized software according to incoming MIDI signals from the console. The console is connected via MIDI to a PC and the sound is generated using a special program (Hauptwerk, Swellinq, GrandOrgue). Top sound quality is ensured at a reasonable price. Because the recorded sounds of individual pipes of real organs are played back, the resulting sound is very faithful. This is the main difference compared to common digital organs of practically all world brands regardless of price. Nowadays, this is a popular and accessible way not only for practicing the organ at home, but also for professional installations, for example in churches.
