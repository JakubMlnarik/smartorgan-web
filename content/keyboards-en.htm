Organ Keyboards

We offer mechanical organ keyboards that faithfully reproduce the tactile feel of real organ action. Thanks to weighted lever arms on every key, each key provides not only the distinctive Druckpunkt (touch point) but also the inertial resistance of a true organ keyboard — an experience that spring-loaded electronic keyboards cannot replicate.

The keys are made of wood, the frame is anodized aluminium. The construction is designed for easy stacking of up to four manuals and is maintenance-free — no lubrication, no adjustment over time. All settings are accessible from the top.

What is a kinetic keyboard?

A kinetic keyboard differs from ordinary MIDI keyboards in that each key has its own lever mechanism with a weight. When you press a key, you first feel a light free travel, then the Druckpunkt (touch point), followed by inertial resistance — just like a real organ action. This mechanical feel is fully adjustable and can be tailored to each organist's preference.

For organists accustomed to mechanical instruments, this feels completely natural. For pianists or electronic organ players, it is an entry into the world of authentic organ playing.
