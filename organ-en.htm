We build complete digital organs and components for MIDI consoles. Our modular design means the console can be easily disassembled and moved without trouble. Every instrument is made to order, so we can match your specific requirements for appearance, materials, and technical features.

Our organs use real pipe samples — we record every pipe from a genuine organ (with samples several seconds long) and play them back via MIDI using specialized software. This gives our instruments an authentic, rich sound that sets them apart from any standard digital organ, regardless of price.

We also supply all the parts needed to build your own MIDI organ console. Today, it's easier than ever to practice the organ at home: connect a console to a PC via MIDI and use software like Hauptwerk or GrandOrgue to generate the sound. You get professional quality at a reasonable price.

Pedalboards

Our pedalboards follow the German BDO organ standard — parallel and concave, with a C–f1 range (30 notes). The construction is designed to minimize noise (impacts, knocking) and side play during playing.

We supply pedalboards with a spruce frame and beech keys, finished to your preference. Magnetic contacts provide reliable, long-lasting key switching. The pedalboard can be built into a console, used standalone for practice with a basic electronic keyboard, or paired with our Cecilia organ module. We offer both a solid-frame version and a fully dismountable version — ideal for long-distance shipping.

Keybeds

Our manual blocks use high-quality FATAR keyboards. The standard configuration is a five-octave manual with balanced plastic keys and touch simulation. You can arrange any number of manuals; two manuals is the typical setup. The block can also include combination buttons, pedalboard lighting with a desk aperture, and a music stand. On request, we supply a classical wooden-frame music stand, optionally with lighting.

Desk

The desk design follows the German BDO organ standard for optimal ergonomics and easy physical access. The fully equipped version includes metal foot pistons for switching free combinations, plus pedals for crescendo and expression. The pedalboard fits under the footrest, while manual and registration controls attach to the desk with locks. All organ parts connect via hidden connectors built into the desk.
