Introduction

Welcome to our website. We are designers and developers of parts and advanced electronic systems for virtual pipe organs. We have created own high quality embedded virtual organ system based on real samples. It is a great alternative for solutions based on a PC with special VPO software. We use this system in our sound modules for demanding users and in compact digital organs.
